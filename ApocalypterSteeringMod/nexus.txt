Apocalypter Vehicle Tuning - Changelog
======================================

Current release: 0.11.3-alpha

An in-game tuning panel for every vehicle in Apocalypter: steering,
suspension, wheel alignment, aero, brakes, tire grip, drivetrain,
gearbox and ABS/TCS assists. All applied live while you drive, all
persisted per setting. Press F7 (or the "Vehicle Tuning" button in the
pause menu) to open the panel.

Need help? Contact @OddlyTugs on Discord for assistance.


New in 0.11.3-alpha
-------------------

- FIXED: the Weight tab's two sliders now show only their own number.
  The coarse row previously read the whole weight, so dragging the
  trim slider rewrote the coarse readout and nudged its handle. Now
  the coarse row shows the coarse part and the trim row the trim
  part, and the applied weight is simply the two added together.
  Dragging trim leaves the coarse readout and handle untouched, and
  a coarse drag never touches the trim number. Display-only change.


New in 0.11.2-alpha
-------------------

- FIXED: the Weight tab's trim sliders. Dragging a trim slider used to
  move the coarse slider with it, fight the mouse, and ratchet the
  weight to +-10000 kg within seconds. The sliders' +-100 kg range
  exceeded the +-50 kg trim domain, so every drag event flipped the
  100 kg coarse bucket and re-added it. The trim sliders now run
  +-50 kg (the real trim domain) and the value is clamped, so a drag
  held at the end of the slider stays put and nothing ratchets.
- Also in this update: the ballast/springs behaviour is final as
  shipped in 0.11.1 - ballast leaves the springs stock so the body
  visibly squats under load.


New in 0.11.1-alpha
-------------------

- Ballast no longer re-scales the springs. 0.10.0's rescale held the
  ride height constant, so positive ballast (real mass) never visibly
  compressed the suspension. The springs stay stock now: the body
  squats under load, and the balloon lift offsets the extreme loads.


New in 0.11.0-alpha
-------------------

- The Weight tab's balloon lift now actually lifts (0.10.0's lift never
  ran - it is applied per physics tick by the mod itself now, and it
  is uncapped: big negatives can fly).
- The weight range is -10 t to +20 t.
- Each axle has two sliders: coarse (100 kg steps) + trim (fine
  adjustment; +-50 kg in 1 kg steps since 0.11.2).


New in 0.10.0-alpha
-------------------

- The Weight tab: front/rear ballast in kg (real mass, centre of mass
  and inertia) or negative-kg "balloon" lift at that axle. Presets:
  Front ballast, Rear ballast, Full load, Lift. Works with per-vehicle
  tunes. (0.10.0-0.11.0 also re-scaled the springs with the mass;
  0.11.1 removed that - see above.)


New in 0.9.0-alpha
------------------

- Per-vehicle tunes: save a tune under a vehicle's name and it
  re-applies to that vehicle only. Config-level for now: one line per
  tune in the config file's [PerVehicle] Tunes section (Apocasetter
  edits it live; a panel UI comes later).
- Switching saves now resets the tuning by default
  (ResetOnSaveSwitch = true).


New in 0.8.0-alpha
------------------

- Max steering angle slider (0 = each vehicle's own lock, up to 70 deg
  for drift-style extra angle).
- Save tracking: the mod detects the loaded save slot and can turn
  every category off when a different save loads.


New in 0.7.x (0.7.0-0.7.7)
--------------------------

- Gearbox tuning fully unlocked for every transmission: the mod runs
  its own shift controller while gearbox changes are active, with a
  Truck 12-gear preset.
- Custom drivetrain layouts: centre-diff mode + torque-split readout,
  a panel UI for RWD/AWD/4x4/6x6 wiring, copy/paste.
- The panel is reorganized by car area: 8 tabs - Steering, Suspension,
  Wheels (alignment + tires), Drivetrain (engine/diffs/layout +
  gearbox), Brakes, Assists, Aero, Settings.
- Telemetry: pinned values, then (0.7.4) a readout list - speed, RPM,
  gear, front/rear slip, lateral/longitudinal g, steering angle,
  throttle, brakes.
- A tighter panel (bigger text, smaller boxes) at every width.
- Fixes along the way: 12-gear gearboxes no longer grabbed by the
  game's own skipping automatic (0.7.3), per-tab Reset matching the
  reorganized tabs (0.7.4), custom-gearbox-at-load diagnostics and fix
  (0.7.5/0.7.6), quieter logging (0.7.7).


New in 0.6.x (0.6.0-0.6.4)
--------------------------

- Wheel alignment: camber, caster, toe and wheel position.
- Gearbox tab (gear count, ratios, clutch) with the note that the
  game's shift logic fights resized gearboxes (unlocked in 0.7.0).
- The panel docks to the right edge and the game keeps running while
  it is open - tune and drive at the same time. Prefer the old paused
  panel? Turn on "Freeze game while open" in the Settings tab.
- An optional live telemetry strip (off by default).
- Custom drivetrain layouts (config-only at first: AWD, part-time 4x4,
  6x6 wiring).
- Panel width/size/transparency settings, a rebindable panel hotkey.
- Copy/paste for presets.
- Fixes: the mod keeps out of the game's post-load spawn wave and
  survives a half-initialised vehicle (0.6.3), and telemetry keeps
  YOUR car with hands off the keys even parked next to other cars
  (0.6.4).


Earlier (0.2.0-0.5.0)
---------------------

- 0.5.0: aero, brakes, tire grip and stability assists (ABS/TCS) tabs;
  curve-editor polish from the playtest report.
- 0.4.0: visual curve editors for the steering tab and speed-sensing
  center return.
- 0.3.0: the Euro Truck preset, Apocasetter integration (live settings
  editors) and ApocaLanguage translation support.
- 0.2.0: the first audit round - every fix regression-tested.
- 0.1.0: the original steering mod (traction steering, linearity,
  counter-steer).
